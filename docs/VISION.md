# Vision

The direction this game's world grows toward, written by the studio from the lead's plan. Nothing here is frozen: the module contract freezes interfaces and conventions, and every part grows its own content toward this. Change it by re-planning.

## World scale

one round lake ~160 m across; the walkable near beach is an arc ~100 m wide and ~30 m deep in front of a grass bank

## Past the nearest building

the dark lake full of floating lanterns, sky lanterns rising, a pine-lined far shore and low-poly mountains under the milky way

## Set-pieces

- a small wooden pier into the shallows near the start
- the character's own lantern pooling warm light on the sand
- lanterns setting off from the water's edge and drifting out

## Headroom

a path around more of the shore, a few resting spots (bench, campfire) where people pause
